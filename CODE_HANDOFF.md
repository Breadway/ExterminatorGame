# Handoff: what is built, how to verify it, what is left

Verified in Unity 6000.3.26f1 through Unity MCP: the project compiles with no errors, all 78
EditMode tests pass, and bot runs cleared rooms through branching map choices, Unknown room events
(shock plates, acid rain), upgrade picks and a live Backdraft fuel-ignition chain with a clean
console. An earlier bot run went through both bosses to victory and into endless mode (room 19).
Balance, feel and non-keyboard input have NOT been tested by a human. See "Known gaps" before
treating anything as finished.

## Running it

- Open `Assets/_Project/Scenes/GameScene.unity` and press Play. The lobby opens first (the game is
  paused behind it). Pick a character, spend scrap, press CLOCK IN.
- Window > General > Test Runner > EditMode runs the 47 tests in `Assets/_Project/Tests/Editor/`.
- `Exterminator > Build Content` regenerates every data asset and enemy prefab from
  `Assets/_Project/Editor/ContentBuilder.cs`. `Exterminator > Wire Game Scene` re-attaches them to
  the open scene. Both are idempotent. Edit numbers in ContentBuilder, not in the generated assets,
  or the next build overwrites them.
- Pixel art is generated by `tools/make_scorpion.py`, `tools/make_enemy_art.py`,
  `tools/make_env_art.py` (needs pillow). Edit and rerun; reimport as Point filter, 32 PPU.
- `tools/validate_code.py` is a syntax-only check for machines without Unity.

## What exists

**Run structure.** `RoomManager` plans a run from `RunData` (25 rooms, mid boss at 12, final boss at
25): combat, elite (hardened, more XP, small heal), unknown (random modifier), shop (heal plus a free
upgrade choice) and boss rooms. Modifiers: swarm, frenzy, hardened, lean. Each room builds an arena
(`RoomLayoutBuilder`, five layouts plus random cover) and starts one wave. Winning offers endless
mode; rooms continue, a boss appears every 5 and tier 3 paths unlock at level 30.

**Enemies (15).** Roach, Ant, Fly, Scorpion, Spider, Wasp (ranged), Beetle (armoured), Larva
(evolves into a roach), Cinder Roach (fire spit), Webspinner (web traps), Storm Wasp (shock),
Acid Beetle (acid pool on death), Siege Beetle (siege tank). Enemy abilities are data-driven:
`StatusProjectile` carries status effects and impact hazards, `EnemyData.spawnHazardOnDeath` leaves
floor hazards. Bosses (Brood Mother, The Queen) are
data plus `BossController`: summons, projectile rings, enrage. `WaveSpawner` is data-driven through
`WaveSpec`.

**Characters and weapons.** Firebug (flamethrower), Custodian (mop), Intern (foam), Electrician
(cables), Scientist (beam). Each has the power and constraint from GAMEDESIGN.md. All weapons live on
the player disabled; `CharacterLoadout` enables the selected one. Adding a character needs one weapon
component, one `CharacterData` asset and an entry in ContentBuilder.

**Run flow.** Rooms no longer run in a straight line: after each clear the map screen offers 3
distinct next sectors (combat / elite / unknown / shop) and pauses until picked. Unknown rooms roll
an event (`RoomEventDirector`): acid rain, web nests, shock plates, fuel leaks, ambushes, supply,
document and hidden caches. Hidden caches use evocative lore room names.

**Progression.** Level paths are per-character: each of the 6 contractors has a tier-1 path at
level 10 and a tier-2 child at 20, plus the shared Blaze/Guardian/Ghost lines and 2 tier-3 paths
at 30 in endless; level 31+ grants automatic character-tuned endless boons. Paths are `UpgradeData`
subclasses (`PathData` with a `characterId` filter). All behaviour-promising upgrades now do
something (`UpgradeEffects`): chain lightning, explosive finale, life leech, second wind (via
`Health.OnWouldDie`), adrenaline surge, scavenger, plus executioner (wounded bonus + non-boss
execute), berserker rage (missing-HP damage), momentum (kill-streak speed), time dilation (enemy
slow aura), venom rounds (stacking poison), emergency protocols (once-per-room auto-heal).
Scrap is earned per kill, room and boss, banked when a run ends, and spent in the lobby on five
permanent upgrades and five character unlocks. Saved as JSON in `persistentDataPath`.

**Constraints.** The Firebug leaks fuel while moving and ignites it after standing still 1s
(`BackdraftConstraint` + pooled `HazardField`, 8s burns that spread). The Custodian's mop has
wall-splat bonus damage plus a HUD guard-breach warning. Dashing grants i-frames covering the dash.

**Lore layer.** Collectible documents (14, torn/redacted corporate voice) drop in document and
hidden caches and open a popup; the bestiary unlocks per-enemy on kills and shows in a third lobby
column; death lines rotate as CONTRACTOR #0447 UNRECOVERED with rare glitch variants; tips rotate
per shift; room banners and the lobby rarely glitch. In-game wording is contractor-based throughout.

**UI.** HUD (boss bar, weapon meter, room banner added), upgrade cards, run-end report, lobby. All in
`Assets/_Project/UI/` using UI Toolkit and `IndustrialTheme.uss`.

## Bugs found by playing and fixed

- The player's `Health` was invulnerable in the prefab and scene, so nothing could hurt them.
- `WaveSpawner` was disabled in the scene, so it never heard enemy deaths and waves never ended.
- Enemies stopped at a fixed centre distance inside the player's collider, and a crowd shoved the
  player across the arena and through a wall. They now stop on collider contact and `ArenaClamp`
  guarantees the player stays inside the arena.
- `OnCollisionStay2D` stops firing when bodies sleep, so enemies stood against the player and did
  nothing. Contact damage now polls collider distance.
- One throwing listener aborted every later `OnEnemyKilled` subscriber. `GameEvents` now isolates
  each subscriber and logs failures.
- Moving the player's transform alone was undone by interpolation; the room builder now moves the
  Rigidbody.
- Notification text was the same colour as its backing plate; scrollbars used the default grey.

## Known gaps (not done, or not verified)

- **Balance is tuned mathematically (tools/balance_model.py) but needs human verification.**
  XP 60 x1.22 (level 10 ~room 10), enemy HP +8%/room and damage +3.5%/room, kill-XP ramp
  x(1+0.1/room), attack speed now scales every weapon, flat-damage upgrade outliers capped
  (Claws/Heavy/Berserker/Venom) and infinite compounders capped (Overtime Pay), Queen 1500 HP,
  mop reworked (0.36s swing, 140-degree arc, stagger, spacing shove, 150 HP). The model predicts
  ~40-65% win rates with deaths spread across rooms 7-25. The Custodian is deliberately the
  high-skill character: the bot (no dashing, poor spacing) still loses heavily with it, while a
  human who dashes and spaces should do far better. Verify by hand before locking.
- **Mouse and keyboard only.** No gamepad bindings, no pause menu, no settings (volume, shake).
- **No audio.** AudioManager is still empty. No hit, death, weapon or music sounds.
- **Mid-run saves do not exist.** Only meta progression persists; closing the game loses the run.
- **Only the Firebug and flamethrower have a human-tested feel.** The other four weapons pass unit
  tests and a code review but have not been played. Weapon visuals are placeholder line renderers.
- The path, shop and elite rooms were exercised by unit tests and short bot runs, not played
  through in a normal 25-room run. Shop rooms in particular have never been seen on screen.
- Player animation is still stubs (`PlayerAnimation`). Enemies are single static sprites.
- Spawning creates a whole wave at once; very large endless waves are untested for performance.
- The bot (`AutoPlayer`) is a debug tool and is not added to any scene.
- `FlamethrowerAttack` still has verbose per-tick logging when its debug toggles are on.
- Existing obsolete-API warnings (`OverlapCircleNonAlloc`, old `DebugError` overloads) remain.
