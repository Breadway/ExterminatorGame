# 📜 The Lore: "Vermin-B-Gone Inc."

> *"We Kill It, So You Don't Have To. (Disclaimer: We Might Also Burn Down Your House.)"*

## 🏢 The World: Corporate Satire
The game takes place in a world dominated by **Vermin-B-Gone Inc.**, a mega-corporation that holds a monopoly on pest control, pharmaceuticals, and questionable bio-engineering.

### The "Incident"
Vermin-B-Gone released a new "Hyper-Gro" fertilizer to solve world hunger. It worked! But it also leaked into the sewers, basements, and walls of suburbia. Now, common household pests (roaches, spiders, wasps) are evolving rapidly, growing to the size of dogs, and gaining elemental powers.

Rather than admit fault (and hurt their stock price), the company has dispatched a legion of "independent contractors" (you) to "clean up the surplus biomass" quietly.

### The Tone
*   **Cynical & Bureaucratic:** You are risking your life for minimum wage and a chance at a "Performance Bonus."
*   **Darkly Comedic:** The horror of a giant spider eating a cat is juxtaposed with a chirpy HR voice over the radio reminding you to "Protect Company Assets."
*   **Blue Collar:** The aesthetic is industrial—jumpsuits, duct tape, retro-tech scanners, and clipboards.

## 👷 The Exterminators

You play as "contractors"—desperate individuals who owe debt to the company or are simply crazy enough to do the job.

### 1. The Pyrotechnic (The Flamethrower)
> *"I love the smell of napalm in the morning. It smells like... overtime."*

*   **Backstory:** A former BBQ chef who was fired for "excessive enthusiasm." He now channels that passion into burning bugs. He uses the company's "Economy Grade" fuel, which leaks constantly, meaning he's often standing in his own fire.
*   **Mechanic:** **Infinite Fire Spread / Self-Damage.**
    *   *The Power:* His flames don't just stop; they stick to enemies and spread to anything they touch.
    *   *The Constraint:* The fuel tank leaks. If he stands still too long, or walks backwards, he catches fire. He must constantly move *forward* into the flames he created.

## 📖 The Codex (The Employee Handbook)

The in-game "encyclopedia" is framed as the **Vermin-B-Gone Employee Handbook (Field Edition)**. It provides helpful tips that are actually just liability waivers.

### Sample Entries

#### **Subject: The Roach (Common)**
*   **Classification:** Class 1 Biomass
*   **Description:** "Standard rapid-breeding scavengers. Now featuring reinforced carapaces due to [REDACTED] exposure."
*   **Company Tip:** "If surrounded, remember: You are replaceable. The equipment is not."

#### **Subject: The Spider (Ambush)**
*   **Classification:** Class 2 Weaver
*   **Description:** "Stationary ambush predators. Their webs are stronger than steel cables. Do not touch."
*   **Company Tip:** "Employees found wrapped in cocoons will be marked as 'Absent Without Leave' and docked pay."

#### **Subject: Health Station**
*   **Object:** Vending Machine (Coffee)
*   **Description:** "Dispenses lukewarm 'Bean Juice' for a nominal fee."
*   **Effect:** Heals 30% HP. Warning: May cause jitters.

## 🗺️ The Contract System (Progression)

Instead of a single continuous journey, your career at Vermin-B-Gone is defined by **Contracts**. You choose a deployment zone from the Mission Board in the Company Van. Completing a contract unlocks "Overtime Mode" (Endless) for that specific zone.

### Current Deployment Zones

#### **Contract #001: Suburbia Clean-Up (Launch Map)**
*   **Setting:** Ruined living rooms, overgrown backyards, infested garages.
*   **The Mission:** Clear 25 rooms of Class 1 and Class 2 biomass.
*   **The Boss:** **The Queen Roach** (Sector 7G).
*   **Endless Unlock:** "Overtime in the Burbs" — Fight until the union forces a break (or you die).

#### **Future Contracts (Coming Soon)**
*   **Contract #002: Municipal Waste Facility (The Sewers)**
    *   *Hazard:* Toxic sludge and corrosive leaks.
*   **Contract #003: R&D Facility Omega (The Lab)**
    *   *Hazard:* Reality-warping breaches and experimental escapees.

### The "Upgrades"
You don't find magical artifacts. You find:
*   **"Stolen Office Supplies"** (+Speed)
*   **"Unauthorized Modifications"** (+Damage)
*   **"Union Membership Card"** (+Defense)
*   **"Performance Review"** (Level Up)
